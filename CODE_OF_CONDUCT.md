# Code of conduct

We want Audience Blueprint to be a constructive and inclusive project.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code respectfully;
- focus criticism on the work, not the person;
- protect private and organization-owned information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and intimidation;
- accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope and licensing.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported through the repository's private security or maintainer contact channel. Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
